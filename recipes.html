Bourbon Balls

  • 2 cups pecan halves
  • 1½ cups brown sugar
  • 6 oz Ale-8 Original
  • 2 tbsp butter & vanilla

Combine sugar, Ale-8, and butter. Heat to 236°F (soft ball stage). Cool slightly, beat until creamy. Add pecans and vanilla. Drop spoonfuls on parchment to set.

Southern Pralines

Gingerbread

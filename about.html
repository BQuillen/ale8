Where the Magic Happens

Winchester, Kentucky — home of the only soft drink invented and still produced in the Bluegrass State.

The Ale-8 Factory

Located in Winchester, Kentucky, the Ale-8 bottling facility has been the beating heart of the brand since it moved to its iconic Carol Road home in 1965. What began as a small-town soda operation has grown into a regional institution — yet it remains family-owned, still hand-mixing the same secret ginger-citrus formula passed down through four generations of the Wainscott family.

The facility is famous for its commitment to returnable glass bottles — a tradition most soft drink companies abandoned decades ago. Fans drive from across the state to swap their empties and pick up fresh cases straight from the source. Every batch that rolls off the line follows the same recipe Uncle Lee perfected in the 1920s.

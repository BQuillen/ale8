The Ale-8 One Story

The Beginning

G.L. Wainscott, known as Uncle Lee, established a soft drink company in 1902 after laying eyes on a carbonation machine.

1902

Roxa-Kola

His first signature recipe, a cola product named Roxa-Kola in honor of his wife, Roxanne, launched in 1906.

1906

The Discovery

Uncle Lee traveled to northern Europe where he discovered ginger beer — spicy, hot to the palate, and unlike anything back home.

c. 1910s

A Late One

"A Late One" — 1920s slang for "the latest thing" — was chosen by contest to name the new ginger citrus soda.

1926

New Home

The company moved to its iconic Carol Road facility in Winchester, Kentucky — the home of Ale-8 to this day.

1965

Cherry Arrives

Ale-8 expanded beyond its original recipe with the launch of Cherry — a smooth, fruit-forward take on the classic ginger bite.

2012

blackberry

Deep blackberry sweetness meets Ale-8's spicy citrus — a darker, bolder flavor for those who crave more depth.

2016

Zero Sugar

The original recipe reimagined — bold ginger, citrus, and spice with zero calories and all the signature Ale-8 character.

2018

Orange Cream

Bright citrus meets smooth cream in this fan-favorite — a crisp orange soda with the unmistakable Ale-8 ginger finish.

2020

Cherry Zero

Cherry flavor with zero sugar and a clean finish — the guilt-free pick for cherry fans who won't compromise on taste.

2022

Limited Editions

Seasonal and limited-edition flavors — Pawpaw, Watermelon Strawberry, Blackberry, and more — keep the lineup fresh and regional.

2023

100 Years Strong

A century of handcrafted ginger citrus soda, still family-owned, still made in Winchester, Kentucky — still A Late One.

2026

To this day, each batch is handcrafted using the same secret recipe passed down through four generations.
